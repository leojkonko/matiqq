THE PROBLEM

Simulation accuracy starts with data quality.

Most material databases rely on single-point measurements that fail to capture real processing conditions. When viscosity, pvT behavior, and thermal properties are incomplete or inconsistently generated, simulation models fall short — regardless of how powerful the software is.

The gap isn't in the simulation tool. It's in the data behind it.
